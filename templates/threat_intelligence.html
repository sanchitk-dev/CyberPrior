What do CVE, CVSS, EPSS, and KEV mean?

CVE is the public ID for a disclosed vulnerability. CVSS is the Common Vulnerability Scoring System, a severity score from 0 to 10. EPSS estimates the chance that a vulnerability will be exploited in the next 30 days. CISA KEV is the U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency’s list of vulnerabilities known to be exploited.
