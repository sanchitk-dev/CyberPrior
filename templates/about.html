Severity is not the whole risk picture.

CyberPrior combines real vulnerability identifiers and external intelligence with sample asset scenarios. Asset importance, internet access, data sensitivity, and safeguards affect the project’s risk and suggested fix order. The core risk score comes from documented rules; the machine-learning models are evaluated on how well they reproduce those project labels. This prototype does not independently predict real-world attacks.
